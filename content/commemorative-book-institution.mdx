# Why Every Institution Needs a Commemorative Book

A commemorative book is more than a publication it is a legacy. Schools, universities, corporations, government ministries, and religious institutions across Kenya are increasingly investing in premium coffee table books, and for good reason. These volumes do more than tell a story; they cement an institution's place in history.

Here is why every institution should consider a commemorative book, whether celebrating a centenary, a major milestone, or simply recognising the value of its own story.

## 1. Preserving Institutional History

Every institution has a founding story: the vision that sparked it, the pioneers who built it, the challenges overcome, and the triumphs that defined its character. But memory fades. Staff retire. Documents get lost. The oral traditions that carry an institution's story grow thinner with each passing year.

A commemorative book captures these moments in a tangible, permanent format. Unlike a website that can be redesigned or a social media page that can be deleted, a printed book endures. Properly stored, it will last for generations.

At Global Village Publishers, we have seen the emotional impact of institutions discovering photographs, documents, and stories they had forgotten rescued from old filing cabinets and storage rooms and given a permanent home in print.

## 2. Building Brand Identity and Prestige

A premium commemorative book is one of the most powerful branding tools available. Consider the difference between a digital brochure and a leather bound, foil stamped coffee table book displayed in a boardroom. Which one says "we are established, we are successful, we are here to stay"?

Commemorative books serve as:

- **Corporate gifts** for partners, investors, and dignitaries
- **Orientation material** that immerses new board members or staff in the institution's culture
- **Marketing collateral** that sets you apart from competitors
- **Fundraising tools** that show donors the impact of their contributions

Unlike promotional items that are discarded, a thoughtful book becomes a **treasured keepsake**, displayed prominently in homes and offices.

## 3. Strengthening Stakeholder Relationships

A commemorative book is a thank you note writ large. When an institution involves its stakeholders in the creation of a commemorative volume, it sends a powerful message: *your contribution matters, and we are recording it for history.*

This has tangible benefits:

- **Alumni relations**: A school's alumni are far more likely to reconnect and contribute when they see their institution honouring its heritage
- **Staff morale**: Employees who appear in a commemorative book feel valued and recognised
- **Community goodwill**: A book that celebrates a community's role in an institution's journey builds lasting bridges

## 4. Creating a Legacy That Outlasts Digital Media

Digital content has a shelf life. The average website is redesigned every 2-3 years. Social media posts vanish into algorithmic oblivion. Even well maintained digital archives require ongoing investment to remain accessible.

A book, by contrast, requires no password, no internet connection, no software update. It sits on a shelf, waiting to be picked up. Decades from now, someone will find it and connect with your institution's story in a way no digital medium can replicate.

This is why the commemorative book industry is thriving, especially in the digital age. The more ephemeral our digital lives become, the more we value things built to last.

## 5. Generating Revenue

A carefully executed commemorative book can also be a revenue generator. Many institutions sell copies to alumni, staff, and the public. Limited editions, advance order campaigns, and sponsorship pages (where businesses advertise in the book) can offset production costs or even turn a profit.
